The Foundation
Born from engineering, built for design

Neuhom is the architectural lighting arm of Camboj Electricals—a company whose story begins in 1965, when Joginder Singh came to Delhi with nothing but determination and a dream. What began as a small electrical contracting operation grew over five decades into one of India's foremost BIS-certified LED manufacturers, driven by a singular belief: Indian-made should mean world-class.

The Neuhom brand was launched to serve a new breed of client: the architect and interior designer who refuses to compromise on quality but needs a manufacturing partner who truly understands local conditions, site constraints, and the relentless demands of the Indian climate.

Milestones
32 years of continuous innovation
1965

A Dream Comes to Delhi

Joginder Singh arrives in Delhi with nothing but determination—starting out in small-scale electrical contracting and laying the foundation for what would become a five-decade legacy.

1992

Camboj Electricals Incorporated

Formally incorporated and expanded into manufacturing, introducing our first lighting products under the Servo brand for industrial and commercial spaces.

2014

From Chokes to LED

As the industry clung to traditional fluorescent fittings and magnetic chokes, we made the shift to LED manufacturing—setting up our own production line and backing it with BIS certification across the product range.

2018

Showroom & Decorative Lighting

Opened our first experience showroom, expanding beyond commercial fittings into decorative and residential lighting—bringing Kalinga and Compaq to homeowners, retailers, and interior designers.

2024

End-to-End Project Delivery

Moved beyond supply to take ownership of full project execution—handling design, procurement, installation, and commissioning as a single partner for architects and developers.

2026

Neuhom Incorporated

Neuhom is formally incorporated as the architectural lighting and project execution vertical—bringing structure, accountability, and design intent under one roof for premium residential and commercial projects.
